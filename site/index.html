01 · The problem

Everyone is building agents.
Nobody checks if they agree.

Agents live in personal Projects and workflows. Each builder brings their own definition and their own spreadsheet. The answers drift, and the wrong number travels to customers, forecasts and the board.

02 · The solution

Concord is the source of truth
your agents call.

Security tools ask if an agent is safe. Concord asks if all your agents give the same answer, then makes sure they do.

See

Connects read-only and maps every agent: who built it, what it does, which definition and source it uses.

Catch

Groups agents by job, asks them the same question, and flags it when the answers differ.

Decide

Routes each conflict to the person who owns the answer. Compare definitions side by side and pick one.

Publish

Ships the decision as an official block over MCP. Agents are rewired; builders change nothing.

03 · Product walkthrough

From scattered agents
to one answer in five steps.

  1. 01

    Connect, read-only

    An org admin authorizes Concord in Claude and n8n. The scopes say exactly what Concord can and cannot do: no edits, no messages, no conversation content.

  2. 02

    Scan every agent

    Concord reads every Claude Project and n8n workflow, then groups agents by the job they do: churn reasons, ARR, discount approval and more.

  3. 03

    Search any business term

    Type "customer health", or even "custmer helth". See every agent doing that job, with its definition, source and answer side by side.

  4. 04

    See the conflict and what's at stake

    $4.8M, $5.3M, $5.1M. Concord diffs the definitions, estimates the impact and routes the decision to the owner.

  5. 05

    Publish one answer

    The owner makes a definition official. It's published over MCP, every agent is rewired, and every agent now answers 28.

  6. 06

    Watch consistency climb

    One dashboard for every agent, open conflicts, orphaned agents and official-block adoption. Consistency score: 71 → 86.
